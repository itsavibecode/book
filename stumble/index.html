      About

      Stumble rolls a random live Kick stream. Set the filters, hit Roll, and a countdown moves you on unless you stop it. Lean it toward small streams with Underdogs and you will meet people nobody else is finding.

      Not affiliated with Kick. Video and chat are Kick's own embeds; the live list comes from Kick's public pages, fetched straight from your browser.

      What is stored. Your filters, timer and theme, the streamers you saved or hid, your notes and tags, and your last 500 rolls. Signed out, that lives only in this browser. Signed in with Kick, a copy is kept by our small sync server under your Kick user id, with your Kick name and picture; we ask Kick for nothing beyond reading your public profile, and you can sign out at any time.

      Analytics. Google Analytics counts anonymous visits and rolls, shared with the rest of bookhockeys.com. Visit bookhockeys.com/stumble/?ga=off once to switch it off on this device for good (?ga=on undoes it).
