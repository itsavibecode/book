    Look it up yourself

    Public tools that index Kick chat, gifts, and bans. None of them need a login for a basic lookup. These are where the numbers on this page come from.

    • kicklogz profileGifted subs, KICKs sent, bans, rewards. The per-channel gift totals above come mostly from each channel's top-gifters list here.
    • lolarchiver Kick toolsChat history by username across every channel, plus timeout history and a subscriptions list. Some features sit behind a key.
    • KickStats chat logsStreamer plus username search over archived VOD chat. Skips deleted and timed-out messages.
    • kickchatlogs.comKeyword search over about five billion archived messages, filterable by chatroom and sender ID.
    • KickHex chatChat lookup. Rate-limited, so retry if it refuses.
    • kickbans.appBan tracking across channels.
    • KickSiftReal-time chat filter and analytics.
    • Streams ChartsChannel-side stats only. Useful for the recipients' viewership, not for a viewer's gifts.
    • Kick channel APIRaw JSON for the account: id, followers, ban flag, livestream state.

    Method and caveats

    • Sub and KICK totals come from kicklogz.com's per-account and per-channel records, which cover roughly the last 30 days, so earlier gifts may be missing. Twigggs and ZuesIRL are counted from Kick's all-time gifting leaderboards; nedx's 18 is the streamer's own count. Follower counts are from Kick's API on 2026-10-10.
    • Share at risk multiplies subs by $4.74, which is what each refund shows on wvagabond's Stripe ledger: a $4.99 sub less Kick's 5%. It is what a full chargeback takes from streamers, not a confirmed total. Only wvagabond has published a first-hand number.
    • "His messages" counts what the account posted in that chat since 1 Sept in the source logs. "Not logged" means that channel was not being recorded.
    • Transcripts are automatic speech recognition of the Kick VODs and contain mishearings. Numbers quoted in them are what the streamer said aloud, not Kick's records. Chat comes from live logs or kicklogz's archive, as noted on each night.
    • Who did the charging back is not something public records can show. Kick Support told wvagabond the deductions were chargebacks and would not say more. Loulz's account is that the gifter reversed the payments himself. The claim that cards were stolen has no published evidence behind it. This page reports both and labels them.
    • Timestamps are UTC on this page. Night pages default to your local time and can switch to UTC or Japan time.
    • Corrections. If you are one of the streamers listed and have a first-hand number, or a record here is wrong, reach out through the BookHockeys home page and it will be fixed with a changelog entry.
