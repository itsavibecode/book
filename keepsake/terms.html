1. What this is

Keepsake saves a copy of a public Instagram post to your own device. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Instagram or Meta.

2. Public posts only

It works only on posts Instagram shows without a login. Private accounts, stories, highlights and messages are out of reach, and Keepsake does not try to get around that.

3. How the files travel

Files pass through our server as a pipe and are never stored. Instagram does not let a browser download its files directly, so our server fetches each file and streams it straight to you. Links, usernames and captions are not logged.

4. Rights stay with the poster

Every photo and video belongs to the person who posted it. Saving a copy gives you no rights in it. Ask the owner before you repost, edit or sell anything, and respect Instagram's terms and copyright law where you live. You are responsible for what you do with a saved file.

5. Takedowns

We host nothing, so there is nothing here to remove. Rights holders should use Instagram's own reporting process. If you believe the tool itself infringes something, contact the site owner via the BookHockeys Kick channel.

6. Privacy

No accounts and no uploads. Google Analytics counts page views and three events (resolve, save, error) with a method or cause label only, never a link, username or caption.
