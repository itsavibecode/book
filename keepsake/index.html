How to use Keepsake

iPhone
In Instagram tap Share under the post, then Copy link. Come back here and press Paste.
Android
In Instagram tap Share, then Copy link. Come back here and press Paste.
Saving
On iPhone, Save to Photos opens the share sheet; pick Save Image or Save Video. On Android and desktop the file goes to your Downloads folder.
Terms Keepsake v0.2.0 Files pass through our server as a pipe and are never stored.

Cookies for analytics

Keepsake uses Google Analytics to count anonymous visits. No ads, no personal data sold, never a post link. The page works either way.
