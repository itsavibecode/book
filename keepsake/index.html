How to use Keepsake

iPhone
In Instagram tap Share under the post, then Copy link. Come back here and press Paste. iPhone does not let web pages join the Share list, so copying is the way. To keep Keepsake on your home screen, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android
Add Keepsake to your home screen once (Chrome menu, then Add to Home screen). After that, tap Share in Instagram and pick Keepsake from the list. Copying the link and pasting it here works too.
Saving
On iPhone, Save to Photos opens the share sheet; pick Save Image or Save Video. On Android and desktop the file goes to your Downloads folder.
