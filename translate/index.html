    About

    Kick Translate shows a Kick channel's chat with translations underneath. Chat arrives read-only through a small relay on bookhockeys.com's side. What leaves your browser for translating is only the text of a line (no names, badges or channel), only when the pill is amber, and only to the services you allowed under Who translates.

    Not affiliated with Kick. BookHockeys

    Cookies for analytics

    Kick Translate uses Google Analytics to count anonymous visits. It never sees chat text, names or channels. No ads, no personal data sold. The page works either way.
